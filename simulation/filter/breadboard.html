Syntsamix · filter test circuit

SSI2144 breadboard

One SSI2144 ladder filter core with input attenuator, resonance and cutoff controls and the output stage, laid out for a standard solderless breadboard. It's the circuit for the eight tests in simulation/filter/BREADBOARD.md.

Before you power up

  1. Check the adapter: confirm with a multimeter that DIP pin 1 connects to SSOP pin 1 (the chip's dot). Most adapters keep the numbering; a few don't.
  2. Notch or dot to the left on both chips: U1 pin 1 at 8e, pin 16 at 8f; U2 pin 1 at 30e.
  3. Power the empty board first: +15 V at 8j and 30j, −15 V at 15a and 33a, 0 V at 15j, 33j and 32a.
  4. With chips in, total current should be roughly 5 mA per rail for U1 plus a few mA for U2 (datasheet: SSI2144 about 5 mA typical). Much more means a short or a reversed chip: switch off.
  5. Pin 14 (Q) and the summer's inverting input (U2 pin 6) sit at about 0 V: both are virtual grounds.
  6. Pin 15 (cutoff) moves within about ±100 mV as RV1 turns. Volts there means a wiring fault.

The pinout and the ladder, output and resonance values come from the SSI2144 datasheet (Rev 3.0, Figures 1 and 3). The input attenuator, drive jumper, resonance limit and cutoff scaling are this project's values. A script checked the breadboard layout against the schematic, connection by connection: no missing links, no shorts.

Off-board wiring

IN jack
Tip → “IN” tag (2a); ring and sleeve → GND rail. A mono TS cable works the same.
OUT jack
Tip → “OUT” tag (25a); sleeve → GND rail.
CV jack (optional)
Tip → “CV” tag (35j); sleeve → GND rail.
RV1 cutoff, 10 kΩ linear
Clockwise end → +15 V rail; other end → −15 V rail; wiper → “RV1 wiper” tag (41i). If the cutoff falls when you turn clockwise, swap the two ends.
RV2 resonance, 10 kΩ
Clockwise end → +15 V rail; other end → GND rail; wiper → “RV2 wiper” tag (3h). Reverse-audio taper gives the nicest feel; linear works.
RV4 offset trim, 50 kΩ
One end → +15 V rail; other end → −15 V rail; wiper → “RV4 wiper” tag (22j). A multiturn trimmer can also sit on the board.
Bench supply
+15 V → +15 V rail, −15 V → −15 V rail, common → GND rail. Current limit about 50 mA per rail. 10 µF across each rail where the supply enters.
