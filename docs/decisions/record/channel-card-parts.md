# Channel card decisions: parts and mechanics

Part choices that make the channel card (`hardware/channel-card`) fit the panel stack and the parts rules: regulators, electrolytics, underside parts, low-cut capacitors, panel pots and the trim stage, button LEDs and chain headers. Split from `channel-card.md` on 2026-10-09 (size); numbers unchanged.

Part of the decision log; the index of all decisions is `INDEX.md`. Numbers are global and never reused. Text marked **[proposed]** still needs the user's confirmation.

## Decisions

110. Channel regulators in D²PAK (`/board channel-card mechanical parts` 2026-10-09; refines item 78, for the 9 mm height limit of item 107). U501 becomes the onsemi LM317D2TR4G (LCSC C12848) and U502 the onsemi LM337D2TR4G (LCSC C232416), both TO-263 (KiCad `Package_TO_SOT_SMD:TO-263-2`; pinout 1 ADJ, 2 OUT (LM317) / IN (LM337), 3 IN / OUT, tab = pin 2, from the onsemi datasheets LM317 Rev. 13 and LM337 Rev. 11), placed by JLCPCB. Each tab is soldered to about 20 × 20 mm of copper on the top layer, stitched with vias to a pour on the bottom; the tabs sit on different nets (LM317: +15.1 V output; LM337: raw −20 V input), so each pour keeps normal clearance and touches no AGND. Thermal: onsemi LM337 Figure 19 gives about 45 / 40 / 36 °C/W for a 10 / 15 / 20 mm square of 2 oz copper (board vertical, free air), TI SLVS044Z gives a 32 °C/W floor and little gain past 1 in²; with 40 °C/W for 1 oz copper on a horizontal card under the panel, the worst case of `power_budget.py` (1.22 W / 1.05 W) gives about 89 / 82 °C junction at 40 °C ambient, typical (0.59 / 0.51 W) about 64 °C, against a 125 °C maximum and the 100 °C target of item 95. The 40 °C ambient waits for the heat budget (`ARCHITECTURE.md`). Both are JLCPCB extended parts: $3 per part type per order (not from an official JLCPCB page). Rejected: TO-220 upright (65 °C/W in free air per onsemi: about 119 °C, and too tall), TO-220 laid flat and screwed to copper (hand-soldered, heat path through the screw), a clip-on heatsink (height, cost), and the fee-free UTC LM317AG-TN3-R DPAK (LCSC C75510, preferred) with the D²PAK LM337 (smaller tab on the hotter regulator, mismatched pair, saves one $3 fee per order). JLCPCB has no fee-free negative regulator (checked 2026-10-09)
111. Low-profile electrolytics (`/board channel-card mechanical parts` 2026-10-09; for the 9 mm height limit of item 107). Regulator capacitors become SMD aluminium electrolytics placed by JLCPCB: C501/C506 47 µF 35 V ROQANG RVT1V470M0605 (LCSC C72522, 6.3 × 5.4 mm, `CP_Elec_6.3x5.4`) and C503, C504, C508, C509, C512 10 µF 35 V ROQANG RVT1V100M0505 (LCSC C72486, 5 × 5.4 mm, `CP_Elec_5x5.4`; 35 V instead of 25 V so one rating serves every position). The 10 µF bipolar audio coupling capacitors C5, C6, C101, C107, C151, C157, C221, C271 become the Panasonic EEE-1VA100NP SMD bipolar (VS series, 10 µF 35 V, 6.3 mm diameter by 5.4 mm height per distributor listings, `C_Elec_6.3x5.4`; Mouser 667-EEE-1VA100NP): LCSC stocks no bipolar SMD electrolytic, so these are hand-soldered or bought through JLCPCB global sourcing. Confirm the case code and land pattern on Panasonic's drawing at layout. None of these is fee-free at JLCPCB (two extended types, $3 each per order). Rejected: low-profile radial bipolars (none under 11 mm at 10 µF at TME or Mouser) and two polarised 22 µF in series per position (all LCSC, but twice the parts and area on a 33 mm card). The master card keeps its radial parts (SX-C-003, SX-C-011, SX-C-012) until its own session
112. Trimmers and jumper headers on the underside (`/board channel-card mechanical parts` 2026-10-09; a narrow exception to the "no tall parts on the underside" of item 107). RV101, RV151 (cutoff offset, item 16) and RV182 (V/oct scale) stay Bourns 3296W-1-503LF (10.03 mm tall, 25 turns, top adjust; Bourns 3296 datasheet) and are mounted on the underside with the adjusting screw facing down, so they can be set with the card mounted in the frame and the bottom cover off. Their leads are soldered on the top side, among the SMD parts, so the item 107 concern about soldering on the bottom does not apply; they sit in the middle of the card next to their SSI2144s, clear of the edge zones where the chain headers and U-loop jumpers run (item 109), within the underside keep-out to be set in `MECHANICAL.md` (keep-out and cover set by item 121), and no deeper than the headers with their mated sockets, so they set no new underside depth. The jumper headers follow the same rule (widened in the same session): JP101/JP151 (filter drive, item 50), JP102/JP152 (resonance compensation, item 71) and J301/J302 (AUX pre/post, item 23) go on the underside, leads soldered on the top side, clear of the header edge zones, each L/R pair side by side with matching labels in the bottom silkscreen; with caps they are about 8.5 mm deep (typical, not from a datasheet), less than the trimmers. The voicing jumpers are expected to change while the prototype is tuned in a real mix and the AUX jumpers per setup, so all of them must be reachable with the card mounted. Needs an easily detachable bottom cover (noted for `/system mechanical`, `MECHANICAL.md` Open item 1). Rejected: setting the trimmers on the bench before the panel goes on with a low-profile top-side trimmer (no adjustment once mounted) and access holes in the top panel (panel layout change, visible holes); for the jumpers, keeping them on top as set-once settings (every change would mean taking the card off its panel)
113. Low-cut capacitors C0G instead of film (`/board channel-card mechanical parts` 2026-10-09; refines items 65 and 73). C9-C12, the four 47 nF 5 % capacitors of the 100 Hz Sallen-Key low-cut, become Murata GRM31M5C1H473JA01L, C0G 47 nF 5 % 50 V 1206 (LCSC C21812), placed by JLCPCB: C0G has no voltage coefficient or ageing, so it is as good as film here, at the same tolerance, and it takes four hand-soldered through-hole parts off each card (one more extended-part fee, $3 per order). Rejected: keeping film (KEMET R82EC2470DQ60J or WIMA MKS2 47 nF 100 V, 7.2 × 2.5 × 6.5 mm per TME, fits under the 9 mm limit of item 107, but hand-soldered)
114. Panel pot shaft standard (`/board channel-card mechanical parts` 2026-10-09; refines item 107). The channel card's Alpha 9 mm vertical pots (RD901F-40 single, RD902F-40 dual) use a 6 mm T18 knurled (split, 18 teeth) shaft, 15 mm from the body face, with push-on knobs: about 13.4 mm of shaft above the panel. The sourcing check (2026-10-09) found CUTOFF 50 k linear and AUX 10 k audio dual in this shaft at Thonk, and RESONANCE 10 k reverse audio as Alpha RD901F-40-15K-C10K at Tayda; TRIM 250 k audio dual exists in no shaft type (a circuit question, not a shaft one). Alpha's RD902F-40 drawing gives the M7×0.75 bushing 5.0 mm long and shaft L from the body face, and its pin pattern matches the KiCad footprints, but the T18 shop pages do not state the bushing thread: check it on the first delivery (M7×0.75 × 5 mm confirmed by Thonk support 2026-10-09, see 115; synthCube notes T18 pots take different nuts). Knobs to choose later from T18 push-on types up to about 19 mm across (for example Davies 1900H clone, 12 mm base, 16 mm tall). Rejected: flatted D shaft (fewer stocked values, dearer knobs) and 6.35 mm solid shaft with set-screw knobs (dearer knobs)
115. Trim stage on a 100 k pot, and the channel pot parts (`/board channel-card mechanical parts` 2026-10-09; refines item 26). No 9 mm 250 k audio dual pot is stocked in any shaft (sourcing check 2026-10-09), so the inverting trim stage (NE5532 U2, pot gang as a variable feedback resistor) is scaled by 0.4: R5/R6 10 k → 4.02 k, R7/R8 2 k → 806 Ω, R9/R10 (soft-clip branch) 4.7 k → 1.87 k, C7/C8 22 p → 56 p C0G, RV1 250 k → 100 k audio dual. Gain (0.806 k + 0…100 k)/4.02 k keeps −14 to +28 dB, the same taper feel and the same soft-clip shape (the knee depends on the ratio of R9 to the feedback resistance); bandwidth at full gain about 28 kHz (was 28.7 kHz); input resistor noise about 4 dB lower; the AD8273 now drives 4.02 k (about 1.5 mA at ±6 V) and the input coupling corner moves from 1.6 Hz to 4 Hz. Pots, all Alpha 9 mm vertical, T18 knurled 15 mm (item 114): TRIM A100K dual and AUX A10K dual from Thonk (SX-POT-011, SX-POT-013), CUTOFF B50K from Thonk (SX-POT-003), RESONANCE C10K Alpha RD901F-40-15K-C10K from Tayda (SX-POT-012). Rejected: a special order of RD902F-40-15K-A250K (lead time and minimum order unknown) and a B250K linear dual with a law-shaping resistor (single source, approximate law). Not orderable yet: no RoHS statement was found for these four pots (decision 116); declarations requested from Alpha, Thonk and Tayda, with Same Sky PTN09x as the fallback for TRIM and CUTOFF (channel card `STATUS.md`). Update 2026-10-09: Thonk sent a RoHS declaration and the Alpha order codes (RD901F-40-15K-B50K-0057, RD902F-40-15K-A100K-0057, RD902F-40-15K-A10K-0057) and confirmed the M7×0.75 × 5 mm bushing; it is Taiwan Alpha's general RoHS II declaration, which clears all four pots including RESONANCE (decision 124)
117. Button LEDs and chain headers (`/board channel-card mechanical parts` 2026-10-09). The seven button LEDs (0805 under the printed caps, about 1 mA from +15 V through 12 k) take one colour per function, all JLCPCB basic parts (no fees, RoHS per LCSC): MUTE red NationStar NCD0805R1 (C84256), PFL yellow KENTO KT-0805Y (C2296), SC SEND, DUCK and COMP BUS green KENTO KT-0805G (C2297), LOW-CUT and FILTER BYPASS white KENTO KT-0805W (C34499); brightness evened out per colour with the series resistors after a test with the printed caps. Chain headers: J501/J502 Würth 61203421621 (WR-BHD 2×17, straight THT per Würth's drawing rev 002.001; Mouser's title says angled in error) and J503/J504 Würth 61200821621 (2×4, decision 100), both RoHS Compliant at Mouser, hand-soldered on the underside, holes drilled 1.1 mm as Würth recommends (KiCad's IDC footprints use 1.0 mm). Rejected: one colour for all buttons (no function at a glance) and blue LEDs (not a JLCPCB basic part, dimmer at 1 mA under a diffuser)
