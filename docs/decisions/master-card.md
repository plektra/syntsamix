# Master card decisions

The master/compressor card (`hardware/master`): AUX returns and sends, compressor and sidechain, master out, master meter, headphones, output protection and the card's power sheet.

Part of the decision log; the index of all decisions is `INDEX.md`. Numbers are global and never reused. Text marked **[proposed]** still needs the user's confirmation.

## Decisions

5. Compressor with selectable/routable sidechain input
6. Sidechain filter is an **LPF** (not an HPF); the main intent is bass pumping
15. Prototype master section has a headphone output fed from the master bus (cue/PFL was first moved to the backlog; see item 28)
24. Mono AUX: L/MONO jacks on the master card. Send outputs give (L+R)/2 on L when R is unplugged; returns normal L to R. Returns have level and a main/compressor bus switch
29. Stereo LED level meter on the master card, always showing the master bus (changed by item 43: it no longer switches to cue)
44. Compressor uses a performance control set: Amount (threshold and makeup gain together), Release, Mix (dry/wet parallel compression), click-free on/off button, about 5 gain-reduction LEDs, plus sidechain LPF frequency/bypass and source select. Attack (fast), ratio (about 4:1) and makeup range are preset inside
45. Sidechain source: INT (compressor bus) / BUS (sidechain bus) / EXT (jack); selected by an SC BUS button and the EXT jack's plug detection since item 90 (was a 3-position switch). Per-channel SC send button tapped pre-fader and pre-mute (ghost triggering from muted channels). EXT input DC-coupled for audio and Eurorack envelopes/gates up to ±12 V. SC listen button routes the filtered sidechain to the cue bus
49. Master meter: stereo, 12 segments per side (-30, -20, -15, -10, -6, -3, 0, +3, +6, +9, +12, clip), measuring the master bus after the master level control. 0 = +4 dBu; clip lights about 3 dB below the internal limit (about +17 dBu internal, +23 dBu at the balanced outputs). All meters (channel and master) are peak-reading with a slow fall of about 1 to 2 s
51. AUX sends and returns levels: each send has a dual-gang master pot (-∞ to 0 dB) on the master card, outputs impedance-balanced TRS at +4 dBu nominal. Each return uses an AD8273 receiver with a gain jumper (-6 dB for pro/Eurorack effects, +6 dB for pedals), a linear pot controlling an SSI2162 VCA (-∞ to +10 dB), a mute button and the main/compressor bus switch
56. Headphone output for the prototype: dual-gang volume pot, NJM4556A-class high-current op amp driver (replaced by the TPA6120A2, item 84), 32 to 600 Ω, 6.3 mm jack on the front or top panel. A dedicated headphone amp chip can be considered for a product version
57. Compressor detector: feed-forward, peak-sensing, log conversion with a matched transistor array (AS3046 / THAT300 class; THAT2252 is obsolete). Amount 0 to 30 dB, Release 50 ms to 1.5 s, attack about 1 ms, ratio 4:1, gain-reduction LEDs at 1/3/6/10/15 dB
58. Power-up/down protection: relays on main and headphone outputs, about 2 s turn-on delay, fast disconnect when the chain voltage drops; AUX sends unprotected
82. The compressor's log detector uses an Alfa AS3046D transistor array (SOIC-14; five NPN, two as a matched pair with VBE within ±1 mV), about 4.50 € from synth-DIY sources such as Electric Druid. Not stocked at LCSC: hand-soldered or consigned like the SSI chips. THAT300 rejected on price (about 12.70 €) and scarce EU stock
83. Main balanced outputs use one THAT1646S08-U OutSmarts line driver per side (about 7 $ each): +24 dBu balanced, full level and no overload with a TS cable. **Cost-cut candidate:** if the budget needs it, fall back to two NE5532 halves per side (buffer + inverter with series output resistors; same +24 dBu, but a TS cable loses 6 dB and loads the cold leg). AUX sends stay impedance-balanced
84. Headphone driver: TI TPA6120A2 (HSOP-20 PowerPAD, ±15 V, 1.5 W per channel into 32 Ω; LCSC C70439, about 1.90 $), replacing the NJM4556A of item 56. The NJM4556AM's DMP8 package is rated for only 300 mW (Nisshinbo datasheet 2025-03-06) but would dissipate about 0.4 W per channel at 1 Vrms into 32 Ω from ±15 V; its DIP8 version is NRND. The thermal pad needs copper and vias under it
85. Output protection relays: three Omron G6K-2F-Y DC12 DPDT signal relays (SMD; LCSC C397194, about 0.94 $): main L (hot and cold), main R (hot and cold) and headphones (L and R). 12 V coils from +15 V through a series resistor, one transistor driver, a comparator and timer for the 2 s power-up delay and instant drop-out. Coil data and contact rating to be checked in the Omron datasheet when drawing
87. Master card power sheet: power ribbon in from the power board, LM317/LM337 to ±15.1 V as on the channel cards, and an L7805 (TO-220) for +5 V because the master's LED load (two 12-segment meters, compressor and PFL LEDs, about 60 to 70 mA) is too much heat for a 78L05. The system star point (the only AGND-PGND connection) is a net tie on this sheet. The frame bonds to it through a ground-lift switch; lifted, through 100 Ω ∥ 100 nF (100 V). Regulator heat is rechecked once the master's load is known; TO-220 heatsinks may be needed
88. AUX return implementation (refines item 51): the AD8273 stays at its fixed G = ½, wired inverting as on the channel cards; the -6 / +6 dB choice is a jumper in the inverting gain stage after it (fitted: gain 1, -6 dB overall, the default for pro and Eurorack effects; open: gain 4, +6 dB for pedals), because switching the AD8273 itself between G = ½ and G = 2 would need four pins per side. The level knob is a 10 kΩ linear pot driving the same three-segment law as the channel fader (+10 dB at full, 0 dB at 75 %, off at the bottom). Mute and duck through a DG413, a unity inverter so the signal reaches the bus non-inverted, and 22 kΩ bus resistors switched to MAIN or COMP. One script draws both identical return sheets
89. Compressor implementation (refines items 44 and 57). Confirmed by the user: (A) log-domain peak detector: precision full-wave rectifier into a log converter on the AS3046D matched pair (Q1 signal, Q2 diode-connected reference current), peak hold in the log domain; (A) MIX is a dual linear pot crossfading dry (compressor bus) and wet (VCA) of equal polarity, and ON/OFF acts on the control voltage, so off gives the dry signal at any MIX setting with no audio switch; (A) makeup tracks Amount at full compensation (0.735 x the threshold drop at 4:1) with a DNP resistor (R439) for half. Implementation, simulated in `simulation/compressor/` (transistor-level side chain, not yet breadboarded): gain -11 after the log converter with the ERA-V33 as input resistor (drift 0.02 dB from 15 to 45 °C), 32.6 mV/dB to match the SSI2162; attack 470 Ω / 1 µF (0.9 ms to 63 %); release from a Q3/Q4 current mirror on the hold capacitor, so recovery runs at a steady dB/s: RELEASE (10 kΩ reverse-log) gives 44 ms to 1.4 s per 10 dB; threshold at AMOUNT 0 is +14.6 dBu sine peak (the channel soft-clip onset), AMOUNT lowers it 30 dB; the ON button ramps the Amount voltage through one DG413 (NO charge, NC discharge, about 20 ms) and, when off, slowly lifts the threshold about 11 dB (reset at once on turn-on), keeping on/off transitions to smooth ramps with at most 1.5 dB excursion. Five gain-reduction LEDs at 1/3/6/10/15 dB on two LM339s. **[proposed]** Q5 of the AS3046D is unused (its emitter is the substrate, tied to -15 V). At full AMOUNT and signal below threshold the makeup reaches +22 dB, 2 dB past the SSI2162's specified +20 dB range: check on the breadboard
90. Sidechain and ducking sheet (refines items 45 and 66). Confirmed by the user: (D) a plug in the SC EXT jack overrides the source selection (detected by the jack's ring switch contact), otherwise a latching SC BUS button with LED picks BUS (pressed) or INT; switching through one DG413 in two stages, no audio through mechanical contacts; (A) the sidechain LPF is a unity-gain Sallen-Key (22 nF / 47 nF, Q about 0.73) tuned by 10 kΩ plus a 100 kΩ dual reverse-log pot, simulated 46 to 510 Hz, with a bypass button; (A) the ducker is trigger-style: when the filtered sidechain crosses ±THRESHOLD (window comparator, 0.1 to 5 V peak on a log pot), SC_ENV jumps to the DEPTH level (0 to 4 V = 0 to 40 dB) in about 3 ms and falls with DECAY (time constant 48 ms to 1.15 s), retriggered by every hit. Implementation: INT = -(COMP_L_SUM + COMP_R_SUM)/2, BUS = -SC_SUM, EXT DC-coupled through a 100 kΩ inverting buffer (no common-mode limit for ±12 V); a follower drives SC_DET (compressor detector, ducker, SC listen); SC LISTEN sums SC_DET into both cue buses through 22 kΩ and pulls PFL_ACT low with an MMBT3904; SC_ENV leaves through a follower and 100 Ω (item 97: a unity inverter with feedback after the 100 Ω, SC_ENV 0 to −4 V, Schottky clamp to AGND). Simulated in `simulation/sidechain/`. **[proposed]** A patched but silent EXT cable still selects EXT; no separate EXT LED (the SC BUS LED shows BUS/INT)
91. AUX sends implementation (refines items 24 and 51): one sheet for both sends. Each send's dual 10 kΩ log master pot sits on the inverted bus sums; 47 kΩ / 47 kΩ inverting NE5532 stages restore polarity (low pot loading and noise); outputs are impedance-balanced (tip through 100 Ω, ring through 100 Ω to AGND). L/MONO uses the R jack's ring switch contact as a plug detector (pulled up to +5 V, held low through the ring resistor when R is unplugged); with R unplugged, two DG411 sections (normally closed) add R into the L stage and halve its feedback, so L carries (L+R)/2. One DG411 serves both sends. **[proposed]** No output coupling capacitors (the DC-coupled chain keeps offsets in the millivolt range)
92. Master out implementation (refines items 46, 64, 83 and 85). Confirmed by the user: (A) the master level uses the channel fader law on a 10 kΩ linear pot (+10 dB at full, 0 dB at 75 %, off at the bottom), through an SSI2162 whose one control voltage sets both sides. Implementation: the I-V stages carry the soft clip (back-to-back 6V2 zeners with 4.7 kΩ across the 10 kΩ feedback, onset about +14 dBu as on the channels) and give MAIN_L/R_OUT for the meter and headphones; THAT1646 drivers with 10 µF common-mode offset capacitors and M7 (1N4007) clamps to the rails per the THAT application circuit; G6K-2F-Y relays switch hot and cold, and when released ground each output conductor through 1 kΩ; coils run from +15 V through 330 Ω (about 11.8 V). An LM339 drops the relays at once when the raw +20 V falls below about 17.9 V (before the ±15 V regulators fall out) and closes them about 2 s after power-up (1 MΩ / 2.2 µF timer); one MMBT3904 sinks all three coils (RLY_N also reaches the headphone relay). The Power sheet now exports +20V_RAW for this. **[proposed]** Only the positive raw rail is monitored
93. Master meter implementation (spec section 4, refines items 76 and 86): per side an inverter and two TL072 superdiodes charge a 1 µF hold through 1 kΩ (about 1 ms attack), 680 kΩ gives a fall of 20 dB in about 1.5 s, and a follower drives 12 LM339 comparators. One 1% ladder from +15 V (115k down to 866 Ω, E96) serves both sides; thresholds re +4 dBu peak at -30 -20 -15 -10 -6 -3 0 +3 +6 +9 +12 and clip (+13 dB, about +17 dBu), all within 0.04 dB. 24 LEDs at about 2 mA from +5 V (about 48 mA). **[proposed]** LED colours: green -30 to 0, yellow +3 to +9, red +12 and clip
94. Headphones implementation (refines items 56 and 84): a DG413 on PFL_ACT selects MAIN_L/R_OUT normally and the cue bus sums while PFL_ACT is low (any PFL or SC listen); a dual 10 kΩ log pot sets the level; the TPA6120A2 (TI SLOS431B) runs as non-inverting gain-2 stages with TI's values (51 Ω series input, 1 kΩ / 1 kΩ feedback, 39.2 Ω output resistors in 2512 for full level into 32 Ω), thermal pad to AGND copper with vias; the third G6K relay (coil on RLY_N from Master out) connects the jack and grounds it through 1 kΩ when released. The PFL-active LED (3 mm) sinks into PFL_ACT from +5 V through 1.5 kΩ. The root sheet marks the unused bus-node pins AUX1_L/R, AUX2_L/R and SC as no-connect (`scripts/reference/root_nc.json`); the master card ERC is 0 errors, 0 warnings. **[proposed]** Gain 2 (+6 dB) for 600 Ω headphones; the cue bus arrives inverted relative to main (inaudible on headphones); PFL LED yellow
95. Master card power recheck (refines item 87; `hardware/master/scripts/power_budget.py`): typical load +15 V 345 mA, -15 V 275 mA, +5 V 29 mA; worst case (every IC at its datasheet maximum, headphones at full output into 32 Ω, whole meter lit) 730 / 606 / 68 mA. From the ±20 V raw rails the LM317 dissipates 1.7 W typical and 3.6 W worst case, the LM337 1.35 / 3.0 W, the L7805 0.3 / 0.7 W (fine in free air). Confirmed by the user: (A) the LM317 and LM337 each get a board-mount TO-220 heatsink of 10 °C/W or better (junction at most about 100 °C at 40 °C ambient in the worst case). The heatsinks are live (LM317 tab = +15 V, LM337 tab = raw -20 V): separate, not touching each other or the frame, or on insulating pads. The power board must supply about 2.4 to 2.8 A per rail for 16 channel cards and the master card (superseded by item 96: sizing figure 3.6 / 3.0 A at full size)

## Rationale notes

- Why a performance compressor: the mixer is played during the set, so the compressor gets a few controls with an obvious musical effect (Amount, Release, Mix, on/off) and the technical settings are preset for pumping. Mix keeps the kick's punch while the rest pumps.
- Why a send master pot but a return gain jumper: the send pot adapts the output to any effect and can be ridden live, so no jumper is needed; L/R mismatch of a dual-gang pot is inaudible on an effect feed. On the returns, a -10 dBV pedal arrives about 8 dB short even with the level pot's +10 dB, so the receiver gain jumper (set once per effect) covers it without running the VCA at high, noisy gain. Returns use a VCA for accurate L/R tracking in the main mix and a click-free mute.
