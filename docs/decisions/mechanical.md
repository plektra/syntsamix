# Mechanical decisions

Frame, panels, strip layout and front-panel parts. The numbers boards must fit are in the contract `docs/MECHANICAL.md`.

Current rulings (decision 154): each entry says what holds now, with later refinements folded in. The full text (dimensions, datasheet sources, rejected options) is in `record/mechanical.md` under the same number; read it when a figure or source is needed or before reopening a decision. Numbers are global and never reused. Text marked **[proposed]** still needs the user's confirmation.

## Decisions

55. Channel strip follows the signal flow top to bottom: trim, low-cut, cutoff CV IN and OUT jacks (106, 166, directly above CUTOFF), cutoff, resonance, filter bypass, AUX 1, AUX 2, SC send and compressor bus buttons, PFL, mute; meter (printed bezel, 119) beside the fader
59. Desktop unit: one PCB plus FR4 top panel per strip, rear-panel PCB-mount jacks, aluminium 2020 rail frame cut to length with wedge side cheeks (sloped, 160), chain ribbons under the strips; rear: one FR4 strip per channel (160)
75. Buttons: latching DPDT CW Industries GPBS850L with 3D-printed translucent caps lit by an 0805 LED beside each switch (latching keeps every state through a power cut). Pressed = pins 2-3 and 5-6 closed (GPBS850L datasheet Rev 2; the MPN was first recorded as GPBS850N, the momentary twin: erratum 2026-10-10)
86. No ready-made bar graphs. Channel meter: 119; master meter (2 × 12), gain-reduction (yellow) and PFL-active LEDs: 0805 under printed bezels as well (162, 170)
106. Strip envelope (contract `MECHANICAL.md`): pitch 35.0 mm, top panel strip 34.8 mm, channel PCB at most 33.0 mm wide, control area at most 320 mm deep. Cutoff CV IN and CV OUT jacks side by side directly above CUTOFF (166). Panels FR4 (aluminium a product option)
107. Panel stack: PCB top 10.0 mm below the underside of the 1.6 mm FR4 panel, set by the Alpha 9 mm pots, which fix the panel with washer and nut. Fader screwed to the panel with two M2 screws through 3.5 mm spacers, screwed first and soldered after (the user's rule). Top-side parts under the panel at most 9 mm tall. Underside: 121
108. Each top panel rests on the front and back 2020 rails over the full 20 mm top face (fixing: 120). The panel is at most 360 mm long; the PCB fits between the rails, at most 318 mm deep
109. Master section at the right end, power board beside it. Chain headers on the underside of every card, long axis front to back, at distances from the front set by the channel layout and recorded in `MECHANICAL.md` (master follows). Unit order left to right: channels, master, power bay (162). Since 175: one audio and one power header per card on multi-drop cables, pin 1 toward the front; the IN/OUT edge headers, U-loop jumpers and the mock-up are gone
119. Channel meter: eight 0805 LEDs placed by JLCPCB (KT-0805G, KT-0805Y, NCD0805R1) under a 3D-printed opaque bezel with translucent windows, 11.6 mm tall (flush with the panel top), through one slot (about 5 × 49 mm), located by the slot and two pegs in 1.5 mm NPTH holes. 6.0 mm pitch, 48 mm column, labels printed on the panel; top (CLIP) level with the fader's top end; meter and fader marks not aligned. Position across the strip set at layout and recorded in `MECHANICAL.md`
120. Panel fixing: one M3 button-head screw per panel end on the strip centreline into a drop-in T-nut, plus a 3D-printed key in the rail slot against rotation. Check rattle and twist on the prototype; a second screw per end returns if needed
121. Underside: only the chain headers and hand-set parts (trimmers, jumper headers, 112), at most 10.5 mm deep. Keep-out (175): no other underside parts in a band across the whole card width over each chain header's length plus 15 mm front and back. Frame has a detachable bottom cover on a few captive or quarter-turn screws (rear: into the bottom angle of 160; front: into the front rail's bottom slot, 162); feet on the cheeks or rails, not the cover; cover and frame height checked at the channel layout against header, socket and cable depth (175)
160. Rear and frame shape: the input module stands on edge under the rear rail, its two NYS216 jacks stacked L above R (two side by side need 35.4 mm, more than the 35 mm pitch), held to the rear panel only by the jack nuts. One black FR4 rear strip per channel (34.8 mm), screwed at the top into the rear rail's back slot and at the bottom to an aluminium angle (about 20 × 20 × 2 mm) along the rear bottom edge, which also takes the bottom cover. Rear inside height about 60 mm. Frame sloped: wedge cheeks, single front rail (front inside 20 mm, 162), top panels tilted about 6° toward the player. Input cable a ready-made 150 mm Molex lead (177)
162. Master section, power bay and front: left to right, channels, master (4 strips, 140 mm, one top panel), power bay (1 strip). Chain 1 feeds the 8 cards next to the master, chain 2 the far group, each feed one end of its group's multi-drop power section (175); the chain 2 ribbon runs along the rear floor on clips on the rear bottom angle. Master rear jacks on two flat passive row boards (outputs: main and sends; inputs: returns, SC EXT, ground lift), 2 rows × 6 at about 23 mm, each cabled to the master by one ribbon. Single 2020 front rail (front inside 20 mm, cover screwed to its bottom slot, slope about 6°). Headphones: panel-mounted 6.3 mm jack on the master top panel, wired to the master card, at least about 90 mm from the front. Power board on edge in the bay, hanging from the bay's top strip and fixed to its rear strip (brick jack, rocker); power modules in two sizes (up to 8 cards, and larger). Master regulators TO-220 on 25.4 mm Wakefield underside heatsinks (174), zone at least about 190 mm behind the front rail (176). Master meter 0805 LEDs under printed bezels (as 119)
166. Cutoff CV jacks (165): CV IN (J181) and CV OUT (J182), both PJ398SM, side by side in panel row 3, IN left and OUT right, 16 mm between centres, centred on the strip (each 8 mm from the centreline), one row as before. A patch cable between two cards' CV jacks is allowed: its sleeve joins their AGNDs in parallel with the audio ribbon's ground, inside the unit (`CHAIN.md`, Grounding)
167. Heat: passive, no fan on the prototype. Intake through a perforated bottom cover; exhaust through four routed slots 2 × 30 mm in each channel rear strip, two beside each jack nut (about 2.4 cm²), and where the master and power bay rear pieces leave room; no openings in the cheeks or the top panels. Design point: inside air 22 K above room temperature (57 °C on a 35 °C stage), which covers up to 8 cards; parts and heatsinks are checked at 57 °C air, the master regulators within their 125 °C junction rating (174, on Wakefield 637-10ABPE heatsinks; was 95's 100 °C; zone: 176). Figures from `tools/heat_budget.py` are **[estimate]** until the prototype is measured; full-size cooling (about 24 K passive; a quiet fan in the power bay or less dissipation) is decided after that measurement
176. Master regulator zone (refines 162, 174): the TO-220 regulators on the 25.4 mm Wakefield 637-10ABPE heatsinks sit at least about 190 mm behind the front rail's inner face, where the sloped floor leaves 40 mm below the panel underside (heatsink bottom 37 mm plus 3 mm to the cover); no frame change
