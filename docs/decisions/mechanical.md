# Mechanical decisions

Frame, panels, strip layout and front-panel parts.

Part of the decision log; the index of all decisions is `INDEX.md`. Numbers are global and never reused. Text marked **[proposed]** still needs the user's confirmation.

## Decisions

55. Channel strip layout follows the signal flow top to bottom: trim, cutoff, resonance, filter bypass, AUX 1, AUX 2, SC send and compressor bus buttons, PFL, mute, then meter beside the fader (item 106 puts the cutoff CV jack on its own spot above CUTOFF and sets the strip envelope)
59. Mechanical format: desktop unit; one PCB plus FR4 top panel (about 35 mm) per strip; rear-panel PCB-mount jacks; aluminium rail frame cut to length with side cheeks; chain ribbons under the strips
75. Buttons: plain latching DPDT PCB switches, CW Industries GPBS850N (Electrokit 41012905, about 0.57 € each at 25+), with 3D-printed translucent caps lit from below by an 0805 LED beside each switch. Latching mechanics keep every button state through a power cut, which is a must (for example a power cut during a gig). Illuminated latching switches were rejected on cost (about 4 $ each for the cheapest suitable part, E-Switch LP4). Pressed = pins 2-3 and 5-6 closed (datasheet), so the logic line is on pin 3 and the LED on pin 6
86. Meters use small round 3 mm LEDs, on the channel cards (8 segments) and on the master (12 segments per side). Ready-made segmented bar graphs and square LED stacks were judged too bulky; the bar-graph sample is dropped from the second Electrokit order
106. Strip envelope and mechanical contract (`/system mechanical` 2026-10-08; refines items 55, 59 and 68). Strip pitch 35.0 mm, top panel strip 34.8 mm, channel card PCB at most 33.0 mm wide, top panel at most 320 mm deep (the panel column sums to about 300 to 320 mm, an estimate until the layout). The cutoff CV jack moves from beside CUTOFF to its own spot centred on the strip directly above it (about 18 mm of depth): at 35 mm a 6.3 mm plug body (12 to 15 mm across) beside the knob would leave about 15 mm for the most-played knob. Panels stay FR4 (aluminium considered and set aside for now; still a product-version option). The mechanical numbers boards must fit live in a new contract, `docs/MECHANICAL.md`, changed only by a confirmed decision. Rejected: 40 mm strips with the jack beside the knob (unit and every board and panel about 14 % larger), and 35 mm with a small (about 12 mm) CUTOFF knob beside the jack
107. Panel stack (`/system mechanical` 2026-10-08; refines item 59, adds to `MECHANICAL.md`). The top of the PCB sits 10.0 mm below the underside of the 1.6 mm FR4 panel, set by the Alpha 9 mm pots (RD901F-40 and RD902F-40: 10 mm body, M7×0.75 bushing 5 mm long, shaft L measured from the body top; Alpha drawing SLH-211-414 and 9 mm catalog page 5). The pots fix the panel with washer (0.4 mm) and nut (2 mm): 4.0 of the 5 mm thread. The fader (Bourns PTA6043, datasheet Rev. 12/14: frame top 6.5 mm above the PCB, two M2 tapped holes 71 mm apart, DP lever 15 mm above the frame) is screwed to the panel with two M2 screws through 3.5 mm spacers, so the lever stands about 10 mm above the panel and the push of a live set goes into the panel, not the solder joints. Assembly order (the user's rule): the fader is screwed to the panel first and only then soldered to the PCB. Parts on the top side under the panel are at most 9 mm tall (1 mm clearance). Rejected: tall parts on the underside (hand soldering on the bottom, in the way of the ribbons) and a taller gap on standoffs with pots not fixed to the panel (knob forces into the PCB only); a fader held only by its PCB pins
108. Card fixing (`/system mechanical` 2026-10-08; refines items 59 and 106). Each top panel rests on the front and back 2020 rails, covering each rail's full 20 mm top face, and is fixed with two M3 screws per end (four per panel), about 24 mm apart across the strip, into drop-in M3 T-nuts in the rail's top slot (slot centre 10 mm from the rail edge). The card hangs from its panel by the pots and the fader. The 320 mm of item 106 is the control area between the rails' inner faces; the panel is then at most 360 mm long, and the PCB fits between the rails, at most 318 mm deep (1 mm clearance to each rail; at 10 mm under the panel it cannot pass over a 20 mm deep rail). Rejected: one screw per end (less stiff against twist from buttons near the edges) and standoffs to a bottom plate (a full-width plate and hardware in every slot)
109. Chain header placement (`/system mechanical` 2026-10-08; refines items 38 and 59). The master section sits at the right end of the unit, with the power board beside it. On every channel card the audio (2×17) and power (2×4) IN headers run along the left edge and the OUT headers along the right edge, on the underside, long axis front to back, all at one distance from the front; the channel card layout chooses that distance (in the rear half, clear of the fader) and records it in `MECHANICAL.md`, and the master card's headers follow it. Neighbours are linked by identical short jumper cables in a U-loop under the gap between card n's OUT and card n+1's IN (about 12 mm apart, about 60 to 80 mm of cable). Before the first PCB order a mock-up (two box headers about 12 mm apart on scrap board, a real 34-way cable) fixes the header orientation and the socket crimping that keep pin 1 on pin 1, and the cable length. Rejected: IN and OUT side by side in the middle of the card (longer, crossing cables) and one multi-drop cable per bus (changes item 38, a different cable for each card count)
