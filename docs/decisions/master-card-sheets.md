# Master card decisions: sheet implementations

How each sheet of the master card (`hardware/master`) implements the scope and part choices in `master-card.md`: power sheet, AUX returns and sends, compressor, sidechain, master out, meter, headphones.

Current rulings (decision 154): each entry says what holds now, with later refinements folded in. The full text (figures, simulations, sources, rejected options) is in `record/master-card-sheets.md` under the same number; read it when a figure or source is needed or before reopening a decision. Numbers are global and never reused. Text marked **[proposed]** still needs the user's confirmation.

## Decisions

87. Power sheet: power ribbon in from the power board; LM317/LM337 to ±15.1 V as on the channel; L7805 (TO-220) for +5 V (LED load too high for a 78L05). The system star point (only AGND-PGND join) is a net tie here; the frame bonds to it through the ground-lift switch, lifted through 100 Ω ∥ 100 nF (100 V). Heatsinks: 95
88. AUX returns (one script draws both sheets): G = ½ inverting receiver (148), then an inverting gain stage with the PRO/PEDAL jumper (fitted: −6 dB overall, default; open: +6 dB). Level: 10 kΩ linear pot on the channel's 3-segment law (+10 dB full, 0 dB at 75 %, off at the bottom; 103, 144), duck into the summer's virtual earth (97). Mute and duck through a DG413, a unity inverter, 22 kΩ bus resistors switched to MAIN or COMP
89. Compressor: log-domain peak detector (precision full-wave rectifier, log converter on the AS3046D matched pair, peak hold in the log domain), 32.6 mV/dB to match the SSI2162; attack 470 Ω / 1 µF (0.9 ms); release from a current mirror on the hold capacitor at a steady dB/s, RELEASE 44 ms to 1.4 s per 10 dB (131); threshold at AMOUNT 0 about +14.6 dBu peak, AMOUNT lowers it 30 dB. MIX is a dual linear pot crossfading dry and wet of equal polarity; ON/OFF ramps the Amount voltage through a DG413 (about 20 ms), so off is dry at any MIX with no audio switch. Makeup tracks Amount at full compensation (0.735 × at 4:1), DNP R439 for half; if the +22 dB makeup misbehaves, fit R439 = 330 kΩ. Five GR LEDs on two LM339s (147). AS3046D Q5 unused. Detector without tempco on the prototype (149). Simulated in `simulation/compressor/`, not yet breadboarded
90. Sidechain and ducking: a plug in SC EXT (ring switch contact) overrides the source; otherwise the latching SC BUS button picks BUS (pressed) or INT, switched by one DG413. INT = −(COMP_L + COMP_R)/2, BUS = −SC_SUM, EXT DC-coupled through a 100 kΩ inverting buffer; a follower drives SC_DET. SC LPF: unity-gain Sallen-Key, 10 kΩ plus a 100 kΩ dual pot (B100K, 138), Butterworth (147), about 44-478 Hz, with bypass. Ducker is trigger-style: when SC crosses ±THRESHOLD (window comparator, 0.1-5 V peak), SC_ENV jumps to DEPTH (0 to −4 V) in about 3 ms and falls with DECAY (48 ms to 1.15 s), retriggered by every hit (drive: 104). SC LISTEN sums SC_DET into both cue buses through 22 kΩ and pulls PFL_ACT low. A patched silent EXT cable still selects EXT; no separate EXT LED
91. AUX sends (one sheet): dual 10 kΩ log master pot per send on the inverted bus sums, 47 k / 47 k inverting NE5532 stages, impedance-balanced outputs (100 Ω on tip and to AGND on ring), no output coupling capacitors. The R jack's ring switch detects R unplugged; two NC DG411 sections then add R into L and halve its feedback (L = (L+R)/2). One DG411 serves both sends
92. Master out: the master level uses the channel fader law on a 10 kΩ linear pot through one SSI2162 control voltage for both sides (103, 144). The I-V stages carry the soft clip (back-to-back 6V2 zeners with 4.7 kΩ across 10 kΩ, onset about +14 dBu) and give MAIN_L/R_OUT for meter and headphones. DRV135UA drivers (125) with 10 µF sense capacitors and 1N4007 rail clamps. G6K-2F-Y relays switch hot and cold, grounding each conductor through 1 kΩ when released; coils from +15 V through 330 Ω, one MMBT3904 sinks all three. An LM339 drops the relays at once when either raw rail weakens past about ±17.9 V (128) and closes them about 2 s after power-up (1 MΩ / 2.2 µF)
93. Master meter: per side an inverter and two TL072 superdiodes charge 1 µF through 1 kΩ (about 1 ms attack), 680 kΩ fall (20 dB in about 1.5 s), a follower into 12 LM339 comparators on one ladder from +15 V (values 146). 24 LEDs at about 2 mA from +5 V: green −30 to 0, yellow +3 to +9, red +12 and clip; LED form settled with the master panel in `/system mechanical`
94. Headphones: a DG413 on PFL_ACT selects MAIN_L/R_OUT or the cue bus (while any PFL or SC LISTEN is active); dual 10 kΩ log pot; TPA6120A2 as non-inverting gain-2 stages with TI's values (51 Ω in, 1 k / 1 k, 39.2 Ω 2512 outputs), thermal pad to AGND copper with vias; the third G6K relay (on RLY_N) connects the jack, grounding it through 1 kΩ when released. PFL-active LED (yellow) sinks into PFL_ACT from +5 V through 1.5 kΩ. Cue arrives inverted relative to main (inaudible). Unused root bus pins are no-connect (`scripts/reference/root_nc.json`)
95. Power: the LM317 and LM337 each get a board-mount TO-220 heatsink of 10 °C/W or better (junction at most about 100 °C at 40 °C ambient worst case). Heatsinks are live (+15 V and raw −20 V): separate, not touching each other or the frame, or on insulating pads. Load figures: `scripts/power_budget.py` (current values in `ARCHITECTURE.md`)
103. U206, U306, U705 (pot buffer A, control summer B) are OPA2171s, as channel item 102; the superdiodes sit on the TL072s U207, U307, U706. Bottom of travel about −102 dB
104. SC_ENV drive: DEPTH pot top resistor R518 (27 kΩ) to −15 V, so U505A gives 0 to −4 V and C504 charges negative; U505B is a follower fed back after R521 (100 Ω), so SC_ENV is low-impedance. BAT54T1G (SX-D-014) from SC_ENV (anode) to AGND clamps positive excursions
126. Op amp power: U409, U410, U802, U804 are TL062CDR (SX-IC-024). Superdiodes U207, U307, U706 and the log detector's U407 (offset sensitive) stay TL072
127. Master electrolytics SMD (9 mm limit): the 16 bipolar 10 µF are Panasonic EEE-1VA100NP (SX-C-023, hand-soldered); C901, C906 ROQANG RVT1V470M0605 (SX-C-025); C903, C904, C908, C909, C912, C755, C756 ROQANG RVT1V100M0505 (SX-C-024). Relays (5.2 mm) stay. Still too tall: U901-U903 TO-220 with HS901/HS902, placed with the master section in `/system mechanical`
128. Relay drop-out watches both raw rails: spare LM339 section U707C compares NEG_DIV (22k + 560R from +15 V, 100k to −20V_RAW; 146) against REF, tripping at about −17.98 V; its open-collector output joins UV_O. The Power sheet exports −20V_RAW. U707D spare
131. RELEASE pot RV402 is a C100K (Alpha RD901F-40-15K-C100K, Tayda, SX-POT-014) with R425 6.2 kΩ (SX-R-091): within Alpha's 0.02 W non-B rating, release law unchanged
138. SC LPF pot RV501: Alpha RD902F-40-15K-B100K-0057 dual B100K (Thonk, SX-POT-015; confirm the order code); no circuit change, linear law puts more of the knob on the bass. The other non-B master pots are within rating
144. The AUX return and master level laws use the channel's E24 values and pairs (one tested set on both boards); R232/R332 30 kΩ (153). Within 0.5 dB of the E96 law, bottom about −100 dB
146. Master meter ladder R811-R823: 16k, 1k8, 4k7, 3k, 2k4, 1k5, 1k2, 750, 750, 510, 300, 270, 120 (fee-free 0805, about 0.45 mA), thresholds within 0.14 dB. R732 = 22k + R737 560R
147. Compressor and sidechain on fee-free values: R420 510k + R456 51k; R426 120k + R457 20k; GR LED ladder R443 470k, R445 4k3 (LEDs at about 1/2.9/5.8/9.9/14.9 dB); SC LPF C503 + C521 two 47n C0G in series (Q 0.707, −3 dB 44-478 Hz). Master TL072 symbols carry the ST TL072CDT (C6961). OPA2171 stays (LM358 rejected), no ceramics on the LM337 output
148. AUX return receivers: TL072CDT G = ½ difference amplifier per side as channel item 143; R201-R204 / R301-R304 10k 0.1 % (first half of each 20k leg), R245-R252 / R345-R352 10k 0.1 % (SX-R-107). Still inverting, PRO/PEDAL stage unchanged; CMRR ≥ 51.5 dB. The AD8273 leaves the project
149. Compressor R421 is a plain 1k 0805 (SX-R-035); no detector temperature compensation on the prototype (threshold moves about 2 dB from 15 to 45 °C). R421 stays next to U402 so a tempco part can return; compensation is a backlog item
