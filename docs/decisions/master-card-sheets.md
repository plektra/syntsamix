# Master card decisions: sheet implementations

How each sheet of the master card (`hardware/master`) implements the scope and part choices in `master-card.md`: power sheet, AUX returns and sends, compressor, sidechain, master out, meter, headphones. Split from `master-card.md` on 2026-10-08 (size); numbers unchanged.

Part of the decision log; the index of all decisions is `INDEX.md`. Numbers are global and never reused. Text marked **[proposed]** still needs the user's confirmation.

## Decisions

87. Master card power sheet: power ribbon in from the power board, LM317/LM337 to ±15.1 V as on the channel cards, and an L7805 (TO-220) for +5 V because the master's LED load (two 12-segment meters, compressor and PFL LEDs, about 60 to 70 mA) is too much heat for a 78L05. The system star point (the only AGND-PGND connection) is a net tie on this sheet. The frame bonds to it through a ground-lift switch; lifted, through 100 Ω ∥ 100 nF (100 V). Regulator heat is rechecked once the master's load is known; TO-220 heatsinks may be needed
88. AUX return implementation (refines item 51): the AD8273 stays at its fixed G = ½, wired inverting as on the channel cards; the -6 / +6 dB choice is a jumper in the inverting gain stage after it (fitted: gain 1, -6 dB overall, the default for pro and Eurorack effects; open: gain 4, +6 dB for pedals), because switching the AD8273 itself between G = ½ and G = 2 would need four pins per side. The level knob is a 10 kΩ linear pot driving the same three-segment law as the channel fader (+10 dB at full, 0 dB at 75 %, off at the bottom; buffer and summer on an OPA2171 since item 103, duck input into the summer's virtual earth since item 97). Mute and duck through a DG413, a unity inverter so the signal reaches the bus non-inverted, and 22 kΩ bus resistors switched to MAIN or COMP. One script draws both identical return sheets
89. Compressor implementation (refines items 44 and 57). Confirmed by the user: (A) log-domain peak detector: precision full-wave rectifier into a log converter on the AS3046D matched pair (Q1 signal, Q2 diode-connected reference current), peak hold in the log domain; (A) MIX is a dual linear pot crossfading dry (compressor bus) and wet (VCA) of equal polarity, and ON/OFF acts on the control voltage, so off gives the dry signal at any MIX setting with no audio switch; (A) makeup tracks Amount at full compensation (0.735 x the threshold drop at 4:1) with a DNP resistor (R439) for half. Implementation, simulated in `simulation/compressor/` (transistor-level side chain, not yet breadboarded): gain -11 after the log converter with the ERA-V33 as input resistor (drift 0.02 dB from 15 to 45 °C), 32.6 mV/dB to match the SSI2162; attack 470 Ω / 1 µF (0.9 ms to 63 %); release from a Q3/Q4 current mirror on the hold capacitor, so recovery runs at a steady dB/s: RELEASE (10 kΩ reverse-log) gives 44 ms to 1.4 s per 10 dB; threshold at AMOUNT 0 is +14.6 dBu sine peak (the channel soft-clip onset), AMOUNT lowers it 30 dB; the ON button ramps the Amount voltage through one DG413 (NO charge, NC discharge, about 20 ms) and, when off, slowly lifts the threshold about 11 dB (reset at once on turn-on), keeping on/off transitions to smooth ramps with at most 1.5 dB excursion. Five gain-reduction LEDs at 1/3/6/10/15 dB on two LM339s. **Confirmed 2026-10-09** (`/board master`; the user delegated the open points to Claude's recommendation): Q5 of the AS3046D is unused: Alfa's datasheet (2019 v.2) ties its emitter to the substrate (pin 13, to the most negative point), and B5 sits on −15 V with C5 open, so it cannot conduct. At full AMOUNT and signal below threshold the makeup reaches +22 dB, 2 dB past the SSI2162's specified +20 dB (Rev 1.2 specifies nothing beyond it; the control voltage of about −0.73 V is well inside its V− to V+ absolute maximum): kept, and heard on the breadboard; if it misbehaves, fit R439 = 330 kΩ (the T-network then gives 0.667, makeup capped at +20 dB)
90. Sidechain and ducking sheet (refines items 45 and 66). Confirmed by the user: (D) a plug in the SC EXT jack overrides the source selection (detected by the jack's ring switch contact), otherwise a latching SC BUS button with LED picks BUS (pressed) or INT; switching through one DG413 in two stages, no audio through mechanical contacts; (A) the sidechain LPF is a unity-gain Sallen-Key (22 nF / 47 nF, Q about 0.73) tuned by 10 kΩ plus a 100 kΩ dual reverse-log pot, simulated 46 to 510 Hz, with a bypass button; (A) the ducker is trigger-style: when the filtered sidechain crosses ±THRESHOLD (window comparator, 0.1 to 5 V peak on a log pot), SC_ENV jumps to the DEPTH level (0 to 4 V = 0 to 40 dB) in about 3 ms and falls with DECAY (time constant 48 ms to 1.15 s), retriggered by every hit. Implementation: INT = -(COMP_L_SUM + COMP_R_SUM)/2, BUS = -SC_SUM, EXT DC-coupled through a 100 kΩ inverting buffer (no common-mode limit for ±12 V); a follower drives SC_DET (compressor detector, ducker, SC listen); SC LISTEN sums SC_DET into both cue buses through 22 kΩ and pulls PFL_ACT low with an MMBT3904; SC_ENV leaves through a follower and 100 Ω (items 97 and 104: DEPTH negative, the follower fed back after the 100 Ω, SC_ENV 0 to −4 V, BAT54 clamp to AGND). Simulated in `simulation/sidechain/`. **Confirmed 2026-10-09** (`/board master`; the user delegated the open points to Claude's recommendation): a patched but silent EXT cable still selects EXT (switched-jack convention: unplug to return to the button's choice); no separate EXT LED (the SC BUS LED shows BUS/INT)
91. AUX sends implementation (refines items 24 and 51): one sheet for both sends. Each send's dual 10 kΩ log master pot sits on the inverted bus sums; 47 kΩ / 47 kΩ inverting NE5532 stages restore polarity (low pot loading and noise); outputs are impedance-balanced (tip through 100 Ω, ring through 100 Ω to AGND). L/MONO uses the R jack's ring switch contact as a plug detector (pulled up to +5 V, held low through the ring resistor when R is unplugged); with R unplugged, two DG411 sections (normally closed) add R into the L stage and halve its feedback, so L carries (L+R)/2. One DG411 serves both sends. **Confirmed 2026-10-09** (`/board master`; the user delegated the open points to Claude's recommendation): no output coupling capacitors (the DC-coupled chain keeps offsets in the millivolt range, harmless to line inputs)
92. Master out implementation (refines items 46, 64, 83 and 85). Confirmed by the user: (A) the master level uses the channel fader law on a 10 kΩ linear pot (+10 dB at full, 0 dB at 75 %, off at the bottom), through an SSI2162 whose one control voltage sets both sides (buffer and summer on an OPA2171 since item 103). Implementation: the I-V stages carry the soft clip (back-to-back 6V2 zeners with 4.7 kΩ across the 10 kΩ feedback, onset about +14 dBu as on the channels) and give MAIN_L/R_OUT for the meter and headphones; THAT1646 drivers with 10 µF common-mode offset capacitors and M7 (1N4007) clamps to the rails per the THAT application circuit; G6K-2F-Y relays switch hot and cold, and when released ground each output conductor through 1 kΩ; coils run from +15 V through 330 Ω (about 11.8 V). An LM339 drops the relays at once when the raw +20 V falls below about 17.9 V (before the ±15 V regulators fall out) and closes them about 2 s after power-up (1 MΩ / 2.2 µF timer); one MMBT3904 sinks all three coils (RLY_N also reaches the headphone relay). The Power sheet now exports +20V_RAW for this. Proposal "only the positive raw rail is monitored" replaced by item 128: both raw rails are watched (2026-10-09)
93. Master meter implementation (spec section 4, refines items 76 and 86): per side an inverter and two TL072 superdiodes charge a 1 µF hold through 1 kΩ (about 1 ms attack), 680 kΩ gives a fall of 20 dB in about 1.5 s, and a follower drives 12 LM339 comparators. One 1% ladder from +15 V (115k down to 866 Ω, E96) serves both sides; thresholds re +4 dBu peak at -30 -20 -15 -10 -6 -3 0 +3 +6 +9 +12 and clip (+13 dB, about +17 dBu), all within 0.04 dB. 24 LEDs at about 2 mA from +5 V (about 48 mA). **Confirmed 2026-10-09** (`/board master`; the user delegated the open points to Claude's recommendation): LED colours green −30 to 0, yellow +3 to +9, red +12 and clip (the LED form, 3 mm or 0805 under a bezel as item 119, is settled with the master section panel in `/system mechanical`)
94. Headphones implementation (refines items 56 and 84): a DG413 on PFL_ACT selects MAIN_L/R_OUT normally and the cue bus sums while PFL_ACT is low (any PFL or SC listen); a dual 10 kΩ log pot sets the level; the TPA6120A2 (TI SLOS431B) runs as non-inverting gain-2 stages with TI's values (51 Ω series input, 1 kΩ / 1 kΩ feedback, 39.2 Ω output resistors in 2512 for full level into 32 Ω), thermal pad to AGND copper with vias; the third G6K relay (coil on RLY_N from Master out) connects the jack and grounds it through 1 kΩ when released. The PFL-active LED (3 mm) sinks into PFL_ACT from +5 V through 1.5 kΩ. The root sheet marks the unused bus-node pins AUX1_L/R, AUX2_L/R and SC as no-connect (`scripts/reference/root_nc.json`); the master card ERC is 0 errors, 0 warnings. **Confirmed 2026-10-09** (`/board master`; the user delegated the open points to Claude's recommendation): gain 2 (+6 dB) for 600 Ω headphones; the cue bus arrives inverted relative to main (inaudible on headphones); PFL LED yellow, as the channel PFL LED (item 117)
95. Master card power recheck (refines item 87; `hardware/master/scripts/power_budget.py`): typical load +15 V 345 mA, -15 V 275 mA, +5 V 29 mA; worst case (every IC at its datasheet maximum, headphones at full output into 32 Ω, whole meter lit) 730 / 606 / 68 mA. From the ±20 V raw rails the LM317 dissipates 1.7 W typical and 3.6 W worst case, the LM337 1.35 / 3.0 W, the L7805 0.3 / 0.7 W (fine in free air). Confirmed by the user: (A) the LM317 and LM337 each get a board-mount TO-220 heatsink of 10 °C/W or better (junction at most about 100 °C at 40 °C ambient in the worst case). The heatsinks are live (LM317 tab = +15 V, LM337 tab = raw -20 V): separate, not touching each other or the frame, or on insulating pads. The power board must supply about 2.4 to 2.8 A per rail for 16 channel cards and the master card (superseded by item 96: sizing figure 3.6 / 3.0 A at full size)
103. Master fader-law input range (from /system 2026-10-08; refines items 88 and 92, as item 102 on the channel card): the AUX return level pots (RV201, RV301) and the master level pot (RV701) run from −15 V to ground, below a TL072's input range at the bottom of travel. U206, U306 and U705 become OPA2171s (TI SBOS516H: input range to V−, no phase reversal) holding the pot buffer (A) and the control summer (B, + input on AGND); both superdiodes move to the TL072s U207, U307 and U706 (A: breakpoint 2, B: breakpoint 1), because the OPA2171's back-to-back input diodes must not sit in an open-loop stage. Same part count; about +$0.60 per master card; typical load 340 / 269 mA (was 345 / 275), sizing 589 / 476 mA. The law is the channel's (`simulation/level/`): bottom of travel about −102 dB instead of −113 dB. Rejected: a resistor at the bottom of each pot with a TL072 (law then depends on the track tolerance) and relying on the TL072 not reversing phase
104. SC_ENV drive on the Sidechain sheet (refines items 90 and 97): instead of a unity inverter after the hold capacitor, the DEPTH pot's top resistor R518 (27 kΩ) goes to −15 V, so U505A gives 0 to −4 V and the hold capacitor C504 charges negative through the DG413 switches; U505B stays a follower with its feedback taken after R521 (100 Ω), so SC_ENV runs 0 to −4 V at low impedance as item 97 requires. A BAT54T1G (onsemi SOD-123, SX-D-014) from SC_ENV (anode) to AGND (cathode) clamps positive excursions; its leakage at 4 V is about 0.06 µA at 25 °C, about 5 µA at 85 °C (onsemi BAT54T1/D Rev. 12, Figure 3), negligible on a driven line. No added parts besides the diode. Simulated (`simulation/sidechain/`, 18 loads of 30k1 = 1.7 kΩ): peak −3.91 V (DECAY CCW) to −4.00 V (CW), 90 % after about 3 ms, 20 dB recovered after 33 ms to 0.82 s; SC_ENV never rises above 0 V. Rejected: an inverter after the hold capacitor (its input resistor discharges C504 into the virtual earth: with 100 kΩ the longest decay falls from 1.15 s to about 0.2 s, with 1 MΩ to about 0.75 s), 10 MΩ inverter resistors (leakage-sensitive) and a rescaled C504/RV504 (changes the pot and the simulated decay range)
126. Master op amp power levers (`/board master` 2026-10-09; confirms Power-cut candidate 2 of `docs/ROADMAP.md` for the master; the user delegated the choice): U409 (gain computer and VC summer), U410 (gain-reduction inverter into five LM339 inputs, Amount follower), U802 and U804 (meter inverters 10k/10k at ±7.75 V at clip, 1 V/µs at 20 kHz, and hold followers into twelve LM339 inputs) go from TL072 to TL062CDR (SX-IC-024, as item 123; TI SLOS078N: VICR ±11 V min, ±10 V min into 10 k, 0.2 / 0.25 mA per amplifier, 3.5 V/µs). The superdiodes U207, U307, U706 stay TL072 (open loop when off, as channel U205 in item 123), and U407 stays TL072: it sits in the log detector at a noise gain of 12, where the TL062's larger offset (15 mV maximum against 10 mV) would move the threshold by several dB worst case. Master load 340 / 269 mA → 331 / 260 mA typical (with item 125), worst case 701 / 576 mA, sizing 576 / 463 mA (`scripts/power_budget.py`)
127. Master electrolytics to SMD (`/board master` 2026-10-09; as item 111 on the channel card; clears the 9 mm top-side limit of item 107): the 16 bipolar 10 µF radials (C205, C206, C209, C210, C305, C306, C309, C310, C401, C402, C701, C702, C707-C710) become Panasonic EEE-1VA100NP (SX-C-023, 6.3 × 5.4 mm, hand-soldered, not at LCSC); regulator input C901, C906 become ROQANG RVT1V470M0605 (SX-C-025); regulator ADJ/output C903, C904, C908, C909, C912 and the TPA6120A2 bulk C755, C756 become ROQANG RVT1V100M0505 10 µF 35 V (SX-C-024). All 5.4 mm tall. The relays K701, K702, K751 are 5.2 mm (Omron K106-E1-11) and stay. Still too tall: U901-U903 TO-220 (about 19.7 mm seated, TI KCS0003B, ST L78 Rev 33) with the HS901/HS902 heatsinks: placement with the master section in `/system mechanical`
128. Relay drop-out watches both raw rails (`/board master` 2026-10-09; replaces the proposal in item 92; the user delegated the choice): the power board's buck and inverter are separate converters with separate output fusing (item 100), so the −20 V side can fail alone. The spare LM339 section U707C compares NEG_DIV (22k6 from +15 V, 100k to −20V_RAW: 0.816 × 15 V + 0.184 × V−) against the existing REF (8.93 V); NEG_DIV rises above REF when the raw −20 V is weaker than about −17.9 V (mirror of the +17.9 V trip of comparator A), and its open-collector output joins UV_O, so either rail drops the relays at once. NEG_DIV stays between about +8.5 V (healthy) and +12.2 V (rail gone), inside the LM339's input range; the Power sheet exports −20V_RAW like +20V_RAW. U707D stays spare
131. RELEASE pot within Alpha's non-B rating (`/board master` 2026-10-09; refines item 89; confirmed by the user): Taiwan Alpha's RD901F specification rates the track at 0.05 W for B taper but only 0.02 W for other tapers. RV402 (C10K) from AGND through R425 (620 Ω) to −15.1 V carried 1.42 mA, about 20 mW, over the rating with the track 20 % low. RV402 becomes a C100K (Alpha RD901F-40-15K-C100K, Tayda A-5370, SX-POT-014) and R425 6.2 kΩ (SX-R-091): only the ratio of R425 to the track sets REL_B and the wiper voltages, and the wiper's source impedance (25 kΩ at most) against R424's 2.2 MΩ adds about 1 %, so the release law is unchanged; dissipation about 2.1 mW. Simulated (`simulation/compressor/`): 44 / 353 / 1427 ms per 10 dB (CCW / middle / CW, was 44 / 351 / 1422 ms); track ±20 % moves the CW end to 1.0 to 2.0 s, as with the 10k pot. Rejected: raising R425 alone (to 1.5 kΩ: REL_B moves from −14.1 V to −13.0 V, much further from the mirror input at about −14.4 V, so the slowest release current rises about five times and the CW end falls to about 0.3 s); a C10K with a resistor at the AGND end and R425/R424 retuned (about 16 mW, still near the limit at −20 % track, one more part)
138. SC LPF pot RV501 is a standard Alpha dual B100K (`/board master` 2026-10-10; refines item 90; confirmed by the user): the reverse-log (C) dual 100k of item 90 has no standard 9 mm T18 source (Thonk and Tayda list no dual C100K; a special-order RD902F C100K would need its own RoHS check and minimum order). RV501 becomes Alpha RD902F-40-15K-B100K-0057 (Thonk, SX-POT-015; order code by analogy with Thonk's A10K/A100K codes, confirm with the order), RoHS cleared by Alpha's general declaration (item 124), 0.05 W B rating, audio only. No change to the circuit: the rheostat (wiper to CW end, 10 kΩ in series, 22 nF / 47 nF Sallen-Key) still sweeps CW = higher cutoff. The linear law spends more of the knob on the bass, which suits pumping on the kick (`simulation/sidechain/`): -3 dB at 46 / 60 / 85 / 146 / 510 Hz at CCW / 25 % / middle / 75 % / CW (the C taper put the middle at about 255 Hz); track ±20 % moves the CCW end to 39-57 Hz; no peaking. Rejected: a special-order C100K dual (sourcing and RoHS), an A100K dual wired reversed (CW lowers the cutoff, against every other knob), a linear pot with a law-bending resistor (a redraw for a law B100K already makes usable). Also checked the other non-B master pots against Alpha's 0.02 W rating: THRESHOLD RV502 (10k between 20k to +15 V and 200 Ω to AGND) about 2.5 mW; DECAY RV504 at most about 0.2 mW; AUX SEND and PHONES duals and RV501 carry audio only, about 6 mW per gang at the +20 dBu internal maximum: none needs a change
