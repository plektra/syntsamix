# Process decisions

Tools, workflow, part numbering, licensing, test points and assembly.

Current rulings (decision 154): each entry says what holds now, with later refinements folded in. The full text (figures, sources, rationale, rejected options) is in `record/process.md` under the same number, along with the part availability notes; read it when a figure or source is needed or before reopening a decision. Numbers are global and never reused. Text marked **[proposed]** still needs the user's confirmation.

## Decisions

10. Design tool: KiCad with its MCP server
11. Specification before implementation
47. One KiCad project per module with hierarchical sheets, custom symbol and footprint libraries, ngspice simulation, ERC/DRC, BOM export
63. Part numbering `SX-<category>-<nnn>` per unique part type, registered in `docs/parts.csv` and used as Mouser's customer part number; symbols carry ProjectPN, Manufacturer, MPN, Supplier, SupplierPN; BOMs grouped by ProjectPN (`docs/PART-NUMBERING.md`)
70. Licensing: non-commercial source-available, the author keeps commercial rights: hardware and docs CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0, code PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0 with SPDX headers, repository configuration CC0-1.0 (`LICENSE.md`, `REUSE.toml`)
77. Test points: bare 1.5 mm SMD pads, named on the silkscreen, out of the BOM. Channel pad list in the record
79. Prototype assembly at JLCPCB, planned around the LCSC stock check (`docs/lcsc-check.csv`, `tools/lcsc_check.py`). Value rules now 134 and 137
116. RoHS is strict (invariant 9, 122): a part may be chosen only when its maker or supplier states RoHS compliance, recorded in `docs/parts.csv` Notes as `RoHS: <source>`; parts without a statement are not chosen. Lead-free board finish (HASL or ENIG), lead-free assembly and hand soldering
124. A maker's general RoHS declaration covers its standard parts (for example Taiwan Alpha's RoHS II declaration for every genuine standard Alpha pot). Emailed declarations are kept in the git-ignored `docs/rohs/`; `parts.csv` names the document, date and scope
133. JLCPCB places SMD parts only; every through-hole part (panel parts, headers, trimmers, relays, TO-220s and heatsinks, the power board's radial capacitor and jack) is hand-soldered by the user. Footprints keep hand-solderable pads
134. Values come from E24 (capacitors preferably E12/E6). An off-E24 value stays only where a ratio or accuracy needs it (singly or as an E24 pair), listed with its reason in the board's `CHECKLISTS.md` ("Component values"). Each board's sessions own their value list and review it before layout
136. Cost control: `/cost <scope>` runs the read-only `cost-controller`, writing `docs/reviews/<date>-cost-<scope>.md` with levers (proposals until taken up in a `/board` or `/system` session). `tools/costs.py` (`estimate`, `drivers`, `unpriced`, `extended`) makes estimates from the schematics; realized costs go into the private, git-ignored ledger with `tools/costs.py ledger add`. `/handoff` notes the cost effect of a session that changed parts
137. JLCPCB-placed SMD parts in this order: (1) basic, (2) preferred (no fee), (3) other LCSC extended ($3 per type per order), (4) not at LCSC. A lower choice only where no fee-free part meets the need under 135, recorded with its reason in the board's `CHECKLISTS.md`. A basic part from another maker counts once its datasheet confirms pinout, package and limits. E24 values should be ones the fee-free library holds
151. Models per role: design sessions (`/board`, `/system`) on Opus; `architect`, `cost-controller`, `validator` on Sonnet (`deep` variants on Opus before layout and orders); helpers `part-facts` (Sonnet), `build-check`, `sim-run`, `stock-check` (Haiku) take bounded jobs instead of general-purpose agents. No model switch mid-session. `/board` reads only what its phase needs (rules in `.claude/commands/board.md`); `STATUS.md` holds session state and `CHECKLISTS.md` the checks, part facts, cost levers and value lists; `/handoff` runs the architect only when contracts, budgets, `parts.csv`, chain/bus/header scripts or loads change
154. Decision log in two tiers: `docs/decisions/<area>.md` holds the current ruling of each decision (short, refinements folded in, one reason where it prevents reopening); `docs/decisions/record/<area>.md` holds the full text (figures, sources, simulations, rejected options) under the same number. Sessions read the short files; the record is read when a number needs its figures or sources, before reopening a decision, and by the validator. A new decision is written in both; a refinement appends a new numbered entry to the record and rewrites the affected short entries to the new state. `tools/check_decisions.py` checks that `INDEX.md`, the short files and the record list the same numbers
