# Power decisions

The power board (`hardware/power`): 24 V DC brick input, protection, non-isolated converters to the raw ±20 V chain rails. Chain voltage and power injection (40, 80) are in `system.md`.

Current rulings (decision 154): each entry says what holds now, with later refinements folded in. The full text (figures, simulations, sources, rejected options) is in `record/power.md` under the same number; read it when a figure or source is needed or before reopening a decision. Decision 100 in the record is the power board's design reference. Numbers are global and never reused. Text marked **[proposed]** still needs the user's confirmation.

## Decisions

60. Power comes from a certified 24 V DC brick through the power board to unregulated about ±20 V, LC-filtered, with per-card linear regulation. Power switch, resettable fuse, reverse-polarity protection, locking DC connector, power LED. A linear external supply box is a product-version option (details: 100)
81. The power input section is a separate small power board (`hardware/power`) in the power bay at the right end, beside the master section (162); it feeds the master and both power chains with short ribbons, keeping switching noise and its ground currents off the audio boards. The master takes power like a channel card and regulates locally
100. Power board conversion and parts. Non-isolated: the Class II brick provides the isolation and its negative is PGND (output must float, 101).
    - **Brick:** 24 V, Class II, floating output, R7B locking 4-pin DIN (pins 1, 4 = +24 V; 2, 3 = 0 V): Mean Well GSM120B24-R7B for the prototype, GSM220B24-R7B for 16 cards (145)
    - **Jack:** Kycon KPJX-PM-4S panel jack screwed to the bay's rear strip, wired to J101 (182); check polarity with a meter on the first brick
    - **Converters:** two TPS54560: a buck to +20 V and an inverting buck-boost to −20 V, about 2 A per rail, synchronised at 400 kHz in opposite phase from a 74HC14 oscillator (4.7 kΩ / 560 pF); UVLO start 21.3 V, stop 19.0 V (680k / 39k, 156). Raw rails stay above about 19 V under load
    - **Input protection:** 4 A resettable fuse (2920L400/30GR), SMBJ24A TVS, two SQJ457EP P-MOSFETs back to back (reverse block, about 7 ms soft turn-on); the switch (rear-panel rocker on J102, 157) drives the shared gate; power LED on the switched side, on the power bay's top strip through J103 (168); a pi filter after Q102 (C108, L101, C103) against the brick lead (187)
    - **Output:** one LC filter per rail shared by the headers (2.2 µH SRP7028A, 330 µF polymer, 100 µF electrolytic damping, 4.7 µF ceramic); one 2 A resettable fuse per rail per header. This board is the small module with two headers, master and chain 1 (185)
    - **Power ribbon parts (SX-CONN-008):** Würth 61200821621 header, 61200823021 IDC socket (1 A per contact, two per rail); hand-soldered, from a distributor
    - **Start-up ramp:** 47 nF C0G + 1N4148W from each output into FB (with a 1 MΩ and a second 1N4148W reset) give an 11 ms RC rise, so the brick sees about 54 W peak instead of tripping its hiccup limit
    - **Full size** (about 3.3 / 2.7 A per rail at the sizing figure, 4.4 / 3.7 A worst case): the +20 V buck probably carries it (thermal check); the −20 V inverter and the 2920 input fuse do not. Settled with the full-size supply after the prototype cards are measured (96)
    - Values and derivation: `hardware/power/scripts/design.py`. Input bulk C103 Nichicon UHE 100 µF 50 V radial, sync capacitors 10 pF C0G 50 V 0603 (158). Power switch: rear-panel rocker on J102 (157)
145. Full-size brick: Mean Well GSM220B24-R7B (SX-MECH-005, 221 W, Class II, same R7B plug and pinout, so jack and board unchanged); the prototype keeps the GSM120B24-R7B (SX-MECH-003). At the sizing figure it runs at about 60 % of its rating, about 81 % worst case. One brick and one mains cord only (two bricks rejected as unprofessional)
156. Converter dividers on fee-free E24 values: UVLO 680k / 39k (start 21.3 V, stop 19.0 V), RT 240k (405 kHz free-running), buck compensation 16k. The 240k feedback resistor stays (the ramp capacitor would grow to 470 nF)
157. Power switch: snap-in rocker on the rear panel (Legion SS11-BBIWG-R20-R, SX-SW-003), wired to a JST XH 2-pin header J102 on the power board; it carries only the gate current. On the power bay's rear strip beside the brick jack (162)
158. C103 input bulk: Nichicon UHE1H101MPD, 100 µF 50 V radial, hand-soldered (no fee-free SMD part). Sync capacitors C207/C307: Samsung CL10C100JB8NNNC, 10 pF C0G 50 V 0603, JLCPCB basic (C307 bridges about 20-25 V)
159. Buck catch diode D201: MDD SS54 in SMA (C22452, JLCPCB basic) on a D_SMA footprint (the D_SMC footprint did not match the part); copper pad and a full-load temperature check
168. Power LED on the power bay's top strip: a 3 mm Kingbright WP710A10LGD (SX-D-004) in a Bivar CR-104 clip and ring (SX-MECH-006, Ø4.47 mm hole in the 1.6 mm FR4), wired to a JST XH 2-pin header J103 (hole on the top strip's centreline about 290 mm behind the front rail, 188) (SX-CONN-016; pin 1 anode through R104 10k, about 2.2 mA, pin 2 PGND); the board's 0805 LED D103 is gone, since the board stands on edge in the bay where nobody would see it (162)
182. Brick jack: Kycon KPJX-PM-4S (SX-CONN-027), panel mount, screwed to the power bay's rear strip with two M2.5 self-tapping screws, so plug forces go into the panel and not the board (the user doubted the PCB-only KPJX-4S over hundreds of matings). Four 18 AWG wires, pin n to jack pin n, to a JST VH 4-pin header J101 (B4P-VH, SX-CONN-026; VHR-4N housing, SVH-21T-P1.1 contacts), 10 A per pin with AWG 16 (JST), about 4.6 A per pin at the full-size brick's 9.2 A. Jack and rocker side by side on the strip, both upright. The KPJX-4S and its footprint stay unused
185. This power board is the small power module of decision 162: up to 8 channel cards, two power headers (J401 master, J402 chain 1) with their fuses F401-F404; the chain 2 header J403 and fuses F405/F406 are gone (its converters carry about 2 A per rail, 8 cards plus the master already need about 1.9 / 1.5 A). Chain 2 and 16 channels belong to the full-size module, designed after the prototype cards are measured (96); the two-module plan stays (the user, against one module for 16 channels)
186. Power board PCB: 2 layers, 1 oz copper, lead-free HASL; outline referenced to the panel (the board hangs from the bay's top strip): 177 × 35 mm, top edge about 5 mm under the panel along the whole length, bottom edge 3 mm above the cover, rising over the front 47 mm (about 29 mm high at the front end), from about 138 to 315 mm behind the front rail's inner face; two M3 bracket holes near the top edge (H1, H2, 5 mm copper keep-out) for two brackets to the top strip, no fixing to the rear strip (a `MECHANICAL.md` change, for /system); ribbon headers lying along the bottom edge, long axis front to back, pin 1 toward the front, so the cables fold flat to the floor. The board is built by scripts (`hardware/power/scripts/build_pcb.sh`)
187. Input EMI filter: a pi filter after Q102, C108 (4.7 uF 100 V, SX-C-018) on the brick side, L101 (2.2 uH SRP7028A, SX-L-001) in series, then C103 and the converter ceramics (net VIN_PRE between Q102 and L101, with C102); keeps the converters' 400 kHz input current off the brick lead (about -80 dB against -44 dB, hand model); C103 damps it. No new part type
