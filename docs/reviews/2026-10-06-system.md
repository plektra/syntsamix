# Validation: system, 2026-10-06

Scope: cross-board budgets in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, the chain and input-header contracts on both sides, the invariants, and the cross-board control scale (SC_ENV). First system validation; there were no earlier reports in `docs/reviews/`.

Counts: CRITICAL 0, MAJOR 3, MINOR 4.

Method: built every reference netlist (`*_build.py`) for the channel and master cards and ran `check_netlist.py` on each; ran ERC on all three projects; exported the KiCad netlists of the three projects to scratch and checked them pin by pin against `CHAIN.md` and `INPUT-MODULE.md`; added up supply currents from the parts in the channel card netlist using maker datasheets; reran `hardware/master/scripts/power_budget.py` and the sidechain and compressor simulations; simulated the channel's control summer in ngspice (ideal op amps, 1N4148-like diodes, the resistor values from `level_build.py`).

## Findings

### 1. MAJOR: ducking scale is about 4.6 dB per volt, not 10 dB per volt, and it changes with the fader position

- **Location:** channel card `hardware/channel-card/scripts/level_build.py:59-64` (R215 10k, R217 100k, R218 15k8, U205B summer); the same network on the master's AUX returns, `hardware/master/scripts/aux_return_build.py:50-57` (R230 10k, R232 100k, R233 15k8, and R280-R283 on return 2). The contract affected is the SC_ENV row of `CHAIN.md`/`ARCHITECTURE.md`, decision 72 (**[proposed]** +1 V = 10 dB) and decision 90 (confirmed: DEPTH 0 to 4 V = 0 to 40 dB).
- **What is wrong:** SC_ENV goes in at the non-inverting input of the control summer through the divider R217/R218 (×0.1364). The gain from VPLUS to VC is the summer's noise gain, 1 + R215/(resistors from VSUM to low-impedance sources). R211 (63k4) and R214 (8k66) only connect to a low-impedance source while their superdiode (U204B/D201, U205A/D202) is conducting. When a superdiode is off, its resistor floats and does not count. So the noise gain is 1.11 at fader positions above about 55 % (including 0 dB at 75 %), 1.27 from about 19 % to 55 %, and 2.42 only below about 19 %. The 10 dB/V figure matches the bottom segment only: 0.1364 × 2.42 = 0.33 V/V ≈ 10 dB at 33 mV/dB.
- **Evidence:** in an ngspice run of the summer, with SSI2162 gain constant −33 mV/dB (SSI2162 datasheet Rev 1.2, page 2, "Gain Constant –33 mV/dB"):

  | Fader | VCA gain | Ducking at SC_ENV 1 V | Ducking at SC_ENV 4 V |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 100 % | +10.4 dB | 4.6 dB | 18.4 dB |
  | 75 % | +0.2 dB | 4.6 dB | 18.4 dB |
  | 60 % | −5.9 dB | 4.6 dB | 18.4 dB |
  | 50 % | −12.3 dB | 5.2 dB | 21.0 dB |
  | 30 % | −30.3 dB | 5.2 dB | 21.0 dB |
  | 15 % | −56.4 dB | 10.0 dB | 40.1 dB |

  The same run confirms the fader law itself (+10 dB at the top, 0 dB at 75 %, about −113 dB at the bottom; decision 72).
- **Effect:** at normal fader positions the maximum ducking is about 18 dB instead of 40 dB. Two channels set to different fader positions duck by different amounts. The master's DEPTH scale (decision 90, `simulation/sidechain/results.txt`, "DEPTH 4 V = 40 dB") does not match what the channels do.
- **To resolve:** make the SC_ENV gain independent of the noise gain. One way is to sum SC_ENV, or its inverse, into VSUM as a current through a fixed resistor (for 0.33 V/V that is about 30 kΩ against R215 10k, from an inverted SC_ENV). Another is to accept and document a scale that depends on the fader position. The same change applies to both AUX return sheets. Decide the scale (decision 72) at the same time and recheck decision 90's DEPTH range against it.

### 2. MAJOR: the system power budget mixes a typical channel figure with a worst-case master figure, so the "worst case" understates the load by a lot

- **Location:** `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` power table (rows "Channel card load", "Total, 16 cards + master", "Prototype", "Power ribbon"); `hardware/power/STATUS.md` "Inputs"; decision 95 (last sentence) and the open item in `docs/decisions/power.md`.
- **What is wrong:** "2.8 A worst case per rail" is 16 × 130 mA (the channel **estimate**) plus the master's **worst case** (730 mA, every IC at datasheet maximum). If the channel card is counted the same way as `power_budget.py` counts the master, the result is much higher.
- **Evidence:** channel card part count from the exported netlist: 9 × NE5532, 6 × TL072, 2 × SSI2144, 1 × SSI2162, 1 × AD8273, 1 × LM13700, 2 × LM339, 4 × DG413 + 1 × DG412, 78L05, 7 button LEDs (12 kΩ from +15 V) and 8 meter LEDs (1.5 kΩ from +5 V). Datasheet supply currents:
  - NE5532: 6 mA typ, 16 mA max per package (TI SLOS075K, page 4, ICC).
  - TL072: 1.4 / 2.5 mA per amplifier (TI SLOS080W, section 5.8, IQ).
  - SSI2144: ICC 5.0 / 6.2 mA, IEE 5.2 / 6.4 mA (Rev 3.0, page 2).
  - SSI2162: Class AB 6 / 8 mA (Rev 1.2, page 2).
  - LM13700: 2.6 mA typ with both channels at IABC 500 µA (TI SNOSBW2), plus up to about 0.6 mA of Q current.
  - LM339: 0.8 / 2 mA (TI SLCS006, ICC).
  - AD8273: 2.5 mA max per amplifier (ADI data sheet as quoted by search; PDF not reachable, **UNVERIFIED**).
  - 78L05 bias: 3.6 / 6 mA (TI uA78L SLVS010X, as a stand-in for the ST part).

  Fixed loads: LM317/LM337 dividers 6.25 mA each, CUTOFF pots about 1.8 mA, fader 1.5 mA on −15 V.

  | Rail | Typical (half the LEDs lit) | Worst case (datasheet maximum, all LEDs lit) |
  |---|---|---|
  | +15 V | about 120 mA | about 245 mA |
  | −15 V | about 105 mA | about 214 mA |

  The 130 mA typical estimate holds. The worst case is nearly double. With the same method as decision 95, 16 cards plus the master need about 4.65 A on +15 V and 4.0 A on −15 V (about 175 W from ±20 V), against the 2.8 A and 115 W in ARCHITECTURE. Per power-ribbon pin that is 8 × 245 mA / 2 ≈ 0.98 A, not 0.53 A. The 4-card prototype comes to about 1.7 A (+15 V) worst case, not 1.3 A.
- **To resolve:** write the channel card budget script (an open item in ARCHITECTURE already), using the same method as `power_budget.py`. Then choose one sizing rule for the DC-DC and the ribbon (for example typical × margin, or worst case) and restate the ARCHITECTURE rows, decision 95's last sentence and `hardware/power/STATUS.md` before choosing the DC-DC module. Check the IDC header, socket and 28 AWG cable ratings against the figure per pin that results.

### 3. MAJOR: compressor R421 (ERA-V33J102V, a 0603 part) has an 0805 footprint

- **Location:** master card, `compressor` sheet, R421 (ProjectPN SX-R-021, `Resistor_SMD:R_0805_2012Metric`). The channel card's R108/R158 use the same ProjectPN with `R_0603_1608Metric`.
- **Evidence:** `docs/parts.csv` SX-R-021 says 0603. Panasonic ERA-V33J102V is 1.6 × 0.8 mm (0603/1608), per distributor listings (ibselectronics.com; Panasonic PDF not fetched, **UNVERIFIED** against the maker's sheet). It was the only ProjectPN with more than one footprint across the three projects.
- **To resolve:** change R421's footprint to 0603 in the compressor scripts before the master card layout.

### 4. MINOR: PFL LED current and logic currents flow in AGND (invariant 4)

- **Location:** master D752 (PFL LED, 1.5 kΩ from +5 V into PFL_ACT) and R101 (10 kΩ pull-up). Channel Q301 (MMBT3904 emitter to AGND, base current about 0.43 mA through R311 from the PGND-referenced +5 V). Pull-downs to AGND on the button control lines (100 kΩ: R17, R186, R220, R222, R313 and others).
- **What is wrong:** when any PFL is on, about 2.5 mA (LED plus pull-up) plus the base current returns through a channel card's AGND and the audio-ribbon AGND conductors to the star point. The button pull-downs take +5 V current (PGND-referenced) into AGND. ARCHITECTURE invariant 4 says LEDs and logic return to PGND. `CHAIN.md` does say PFL_ACT is pulled to AGND, so the two documents disagree.
- **Size:** about 3 mA through 17 parallel 28 AWG conductors (roughly 4 mΩ for 30 cm) gives a step of about 10 µV. That is close to −100 dBu, at the press of a button, so it is not audible in practice.
- **To resolve:** either note the exception in invariant 4 and `CHAIN.md`, or drive the PFL LED from a buffered copy of PFL_ACT that returns to PGND on the master.

### 5. MINOR: stale figures and wording in the contract documents

- `docs/CHAIN.md:52` and decision 42 (`docs/decisions/system.md:24`) say "Power enters the chain there [the master card]". Since decisions 80 and 81, power enters at the power board, and chains 1 and 2 bypass the master. The star point is still on the master (NT901), so only the wording is wrong.
- `docs/CHAIN.md:48` gives "about 2.1 A per rail at 16 cards" (channels only). `docs/decisions/system.md:35` rationale gives "estimated 1.3 A per rail". ARCHITECTURE gives 2.4 to 2.8 A. These should agree once finding 2 is settled.
- Decision 78 still says "about 1.05 A per power-ribbon pin ... must be rechecked in the master card design". Decision 80 superseded this, but the text does not say so.

### 6. MINOR: input for the DC-DC choice: about 1.2 mF of capacitance per raw rail at full size

- **Evidence:** from the netlists, each channel card has 47 µF on each raw rail, and 13 µF (±15 V) plus 10 µF (+5 V) behind its regulators that charge at switch-on. The master has 47 µF + 27 µF + 10 µF. For 16 cards plus the master that is about 1.2 mF per rail; for the prototype about 0.37 mF.
- **Why it matters:** many isolated DC-DC modules limit the capacitive load per output and the start-up time into it. Add this to the power board inputs in `hardware/power/STATUS.md` when choosing the module (decision 60: a family with larger versions).

### 7. MINOR: the input-module cable is not defined

- **Location:** channel J1 and input module J3. Both are `Molex_KK-254_AE-6410-10A` male headers.
- **What is wrong:** with a male header on both boards, the link needs a 1:1 KK 254 crimp-housing cable. `INPUT-MODULE.md` says the module is "plugged into a header" but names no cable, housing or length. `parts.csv` SX-CONN-002 says "MPN and mating housing/crimps to verify".
- **To resolve:** record the cable (housing, crimps, length, pin 1 to pin 1) in `INPUT-MODULE.md`, or use a socket on one side, before the input module PCB.

## Checked and OK

- Every reference netlist builds. `check_netlist.py` reports 0 problems and no duplicate references on all sheets:
  - Channel card: input 44 nets, filter 78, level 42, routing 46, meter 46, chain 26.
  - Master card: bus 29, aux_return1/2 67 each, compressor 81, sidechain 54, aux_sends 34, master_out 57, headphones 28, meter 83, power 10.
- ERC on `channel-card.kicad_sch`, `master.kicad_sch` and `input-module-6p3.kicad_sch`: 0 violations each (KiCad 10 kicad-cli).
- Audio ribbon: channel J501/J502 and master J101 match `CHAIN.md` on all 34 pins (AGND on odd pins; MAIN, COMP, AUX1, AUX2, CUE L/R, SC, SC_ENV, SPARE2-5, PFL_ACT). The master leaves the spares unconnected and the channel passes them through. Same footprint `IDC-Header_2x17_P2.54mm_Vertical` on both cards.
- Power ribbon: channel J503/J504 and master J901 match `CHAIN.md` (1-2 V−, 3-6 PGND, 7-8 V+), `IDC-Header_2x04` on both.
- Input header: channel J1 and input module J3 match `INPUT-MODULE.md` on all 10 pins (8 and 9 unconnected on both). R jack normalling: T to TN = L+, R to RN = L−, so R+/R− carry L+/L− with no plug in the R jack.
- Invariant 2 (one star point): no part on the channel card or the input module touches both AGND and PGND. On the master only NT901 (star point) does, plus U412 (LM339 with GND on PGND and spare inputs on AGND; this does not join the grounds). Frame bond is J902 – SW901 ∥ (R905 100 Ω ∥ C913) to AGND (decisions 61, 87).
- Invariant 3: only the raw ±20 V and PGND cross the power ribbon. No regulated rail is on any ribbon.
- Bus summing: channel bus resistors 22 kΩ (R307-R310 and the DG413/DG412-switched resistors). Master feedback R111-R121 22 kΩ ∥ 100 pF C0G (decisions 74, 79). With 16 cards plus 2 returns the noise gain is about 19, which leaves about 0.6 MHz of NE5532 bandwidth.
- PFL_ACT: open collector (MMBT3904) on each channel and on the master's SC listen (Q501), pulled up by R101 10 kΩ to the master's +5 V. About 2.5 mA when low, so Q301 is well saturated with 0.43 mA of base current.
- SC_ENV load: each channel or return presents 100k + 15k8 when DUCK is on. 18 loads ≈ 6.4 kΩ behind the master's 100 Ω (R521) gives a 1.5 % (0.1 dB) error. The sidechain simulation uses 6.2 kΩ.
- Regulator pinouts:
  - LM317 TO-220: 1 ADJ, 2 OUT/tab, 3 IN (TI SLVS044Z, Pin Functions).
  - LM337 TO-220: 1 ADJ, 2 IN, 3 OUT (TI SLVS047L, page 3).
  - 78L05 SOT-89: 1 OUT, 2 GND, 3 IN (TI SLVS010X, Figure 5-3; ST L78L not reachable, same industry pinout, **UNVERIFIED** for ST).
  - L7805 TO-220: 1 IN, 2 GND, 3 OUT.

  Protection diode directions on both cards were checked for input short and output short. SS14 clamps: cathode on +15 V and anode on AGND; cathode on AGND and anode on −15 V.
- Regulator setpoints: 1.25 V × (1 + 2200/200) + 50 µA × 2.2 kΩ = 15.11 V. The 6.25 mA divider current covers the LM337 minimum load of 6 mA at |VI−VO| ≤ 10 V (SLVS047L, recommended operating conditions). LM337 needs at least 2.5 V of differential there: 3.9 V at the 19 V minimum raw rail, 2.8 V at the 17.9 V relay drop-out.
- Channel regulator heat: about 0.6 W typical and 1.2 W worst case in each TO-220 at 20 V raw, which is acceptable without a heatsink. 78L05: about 0.27 W worst case; SOT-89 RθJA 54.7 °C/W (SLVS010X) gives about 15 °C of rise.
- Raw rail minimum (ARCHITECTURE: about 19 V): consistent with the 17.9 V relay drop-out and LM317 headroom at the master's 0.73 A.
- `power_budget.py` reruns to the decision 95 figures (345/275/29 mA typical, 730/606/68 mA worst case; LM317 1.69/3.58 W). Its NE5532 6/16 mA, TL072 1.4/2.5 mA per amplifier, LM339 0.8/2 mA, SSI2162 6/8 mA and TPA6120A2 15 mA maximum per channel (SLOS431B, 7.5) match the datasheets. The master's LM339s all have V− on PGND, as the script assumes. The master has 10 button LEDs, as the script assumes.
- Simulations: `simulation/sidechain/run.py` and `simulation/compressor/run.py` reproduce their `results.txt` (only blank-line differences).
- BOM fields: every placed part on the three boards has a ProjectPN registered in `docs/parts.csv` (test pads excepted). The only ProjectPN with two footprints is SX-R-021 (finding 3).
- SSI2144 (±16 V operating, ±18 V absolute maximum, Rev 3.0 page 2) and SSI2162 (±18 V operating, ±20 V absolute maximum, Rev 1.2) are within limits at ±15.1 V.
